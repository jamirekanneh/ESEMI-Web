Welcome to Elizabeth Sele Mulbah Institute (ESEMI)

At ESEMI, we are dedicated to nurturing lifelong learners and leaders. We provide a supportive, academically challenging environment where students thrive intellectually, socially, and morally.

Our History

Founded in 2011 by visionary educator Elizabeth Sele Mulbah, ESEMI began as a small learning center and has grown into a leading institute for academic excellence in the region. Our journey has been one of passion, persistence, and purpose — educating future leaders one generation at a time.
