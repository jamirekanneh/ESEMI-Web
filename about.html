About ESEMI

Welcome to Elizabeth Sele Mulbah Institute

At ESEMI, we nurture lifelong learners and leaders. We provide a supportive, academically challenging environment where students thrive intellectually, socially, and morally.

2011 Founded with a vision to educate future leaders — one generation at a time.
Our Story

Our History

Founded in 2011 by visionary educator Elizabeth Sele Mulbah, ESEMI began as a small learning center and has grown into a leading institute for academic excellence in the region. Our journey has been one of passion, persistence, and purpose — educating future leaders one generation at a time.

Giving Back

Impact on Community

Beyond the classroom, ESEMI students learn to lead with compassion and serve those who need it most.

Orphanage Visits

Our students regularly visit local orphanages — bringing donations, sharing time, and offering warmth to children who deserve to feel seen and loved. These visits teach that true leadership means giving back, and that even small acts of kindness can brighten the lives of the underprivileged.

Young Girls Program

A safe, caring space where girls learn about personal hygiene, menstrual health, and self-confidence. Through open conversation and guidance, we help young women understand their bodies, care for themselves with dignity, and grow into strong, informed leaders.
